# Brawler Style

The Brawler Style is based on abilities used by Fullbody, Lao G, Wanze, Ideo, Don Chinjao, Sabo, Garp, and other powerful hand-to-hand combatants. This page lists all of the abilities associated with the Brawler Style.

## Passives

All Impact Type Weapons deal an extra 1 damage per hit per level. Upon reaching the max style level 60, Impact Weapons will do an additional 60 damage per melee hit.

## Requirements

This style is automatically unlocked. You are required to be holding an Impact Type Weapon to use all Brawler abilities. Some abilities may have specific requirements that are listed below.

## Level 60: Blue Hole Gekisho

Blue Hole Gekisho is unlocked at Brawler Level 60. The player grabs the nearest enemy and drives them into the ground with overwhelming force before releasing a massive concentrated shockwave at the point of impact. The primary target takes extreme single-target damage while nearby enemies are caught in a smaller but devastating AOE. The ability will not activate if no valid target is detected. This ability requires at least one Impact Type Weapon to use.
