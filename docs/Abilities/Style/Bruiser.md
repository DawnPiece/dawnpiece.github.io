# Bruiser Style

The Bruiser Style is based on abilities that characters such as Gin, Axe-Hand Morgan, Yamato, Scopper Gaban, Kaidou, and other heavy weapon users. This page lists all of the abilities associated with the Bruiser Style.

## Passives

All Bludgeon Type Weapons deal an extra 1 damage per hit per level. Upon reaching the max style level 60, Bludgeon Weapons will do an additional 60 damage per melee hit.

## Requirements

This style is automatically unlocked. You are required to be holding a Bludgeon Type Weapon to use all Bruiser abilities. Some abilities may have specific requirements that are listed below.
