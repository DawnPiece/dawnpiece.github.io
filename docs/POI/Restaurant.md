# Restaurants

Restaurants are found throughout the world. At these locations, you can buy the entire catalog of food and drinks that are in the game. Simply speak to the NPC to make your purchases. Restaurants come in all sorts of shapes and sizes. While some locations may simply be called a restaurant, some will be referred to as a bar, bakery and other types of food and drink shops. Regardless of the location name, the menu will be the same.

## Food

All food prices are based on their tiers, with the price of each tier doubling. Each common food item goes for 1 Belly, uncomoon costs 2 Belly, Rare costs 4 Belly, Epic costs 8 Belly, Legendary Costs 16 Belly and Mythical costs 32 Belly each. You can buy them in quantities of your liking. Please see the [Food Page](/General/Food) for information regarding what each food tier does.

## Drinks

All drink prices are based on multiplying buff level and duration. The buff levels are 1-6 and the durations are one and two minutes. Please see the [Drinks Page](/General/Drinks) for information regarding the drink buffs and durations.
