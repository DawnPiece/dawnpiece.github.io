# Mercenary Guild

The Mercenary Guild is a location where you manage your crews. The entire Crew System is accessed by speaking to a Guild Master NPC.

A player can belong to only one crew at a time. You must leave your current crew before you can create or join another.

What you can do at the Guild Master depends on whether you are in a crew:

| Situation | Available Options |
|---|---|
| Not in a crew | Create Crew, Join Crew, Messages |
| In a crew | Information, Members, Storage, Account, Management, Messages, Crew Log, Leader|

## Ranks & Crew Size

Every crew holds a maximum of 10 players, split into three ranks. Each rank has different permissions.

| Rank      | Max | Key Permissions |
|-----------|-----|---|
| Leader    | 1   | Change crew name/color/description, transfer leadership, disband the crew |
| Executive | 3   | Manage recruitment, members and diplomacy |
| Member    | 6   | Use crew storage, deposit to the crew account, view info/members/log/messages, leave the crew |

## Creating a Crew

Choose **Create Crew** when not in a crew, then set:

- Crew Name: 3–20 characters, letters/numbers/spaces only, must be unique and free of banned words.
- Crew Color: pick from the standard color selection.
- Crew Description: up to 100 characters.

The crew name and color form your server prefix in the format [Crew Name] Nickname.

## Joining a Crew

Choose Join Crew to browse every crew that still has room (crews at 10/10 are hidden). Each crew card shows its name, color, description, member count, and Crew Power. You can search by name and sort the list.

- Request to Join sends a request to the crew's Leader and Executives. You stay without a crew until it is accepted.
- Alternatively, a Leader or Executive can **invite** you directly.
- If a crew has **Recruitment closed**, public join requests are disabled, but Leaders/Executives can still invite players directly.

## Crew Features

### Crew Information

The crew's home page showing the name, description, Leader, member count, Crew Power, alliances, enemies, and creation date. This is also where members and executives can use the Leave Crew button. The Leader cannot leave directly, leadership must be transferred first.

### Members

Lists all crew members sorted by rank (Leader → Executives → Members), showing each player's live nickname, username, and level.

### Crew Storage

Shared storage for the whole crew, working like the [Bank Storage](/POI/Bank) system. Everyone deposits into and withdraws from the same pages. Additional pages are unlocked with Belly just like personal bank storage, one unlock applies to the entire crew. You lose access the moment you leave the crew.

### Crew Account

A shared Belly account. Any member can deposit Belly from their personal bank account, but nobody can withdraw, the money flows only one way into the crew account. Every deposit is recorded in the Crew Log. The crew account will be used for the future outpost system.

### Management

The Leader and executives have access to the following tabs:

- Recruitment: invite players, view and accept/reject join requests, and set recruitment Open/Closed.
- Members: promote Members to Executive, demote Executives, and remove members. The Leader cannot be removed.
- Diplomacy: manage up to 3 alliances and 3 enemies with other crews.

### Messages

One unified inbox with tabs depending on your rank:

- Personal (All Ranks): requests, invitations, and crew-related system messages about you.
- Join Requests (Leader/Executives): players requesting to join, with Accept/Reject.
- Invitations (Leader/Executives): invitations sent by your crew and their status.
- System (All Ranks): general crew events like leadership or name changes.

### Crew Log

A shared history of crew activity visible to all members: storage deposits/withdrawals, Belly deposits, membership and rank changes, leadership transfers, diplomacy, and crew identity changes. Entries are automatically removed after 7 days.

### Leader Page

The Leader has access to the following tabs:

- Identity: change the crew name, color, or description.
- Leadership: transfer leadership to another member. The previous Leader becomes an Executive.
- Danger Zone: disband the crew. This permanently deletes everything (membership, storage, account, diplomacy, logs) and requires typing the exact crew name to confirm.

## Crew Power

Crew Power is simply the sum of all current member levels and is shown on crew cards and the crew information page. It is calculated live, so it updates as members join, leave, or level up.
