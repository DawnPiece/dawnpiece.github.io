# Bounty Office

Bounty Offices are locations found at various islands where you can turn in the heads of defeated bosses for Belly rewards and track your bounty completion progress. To see which islands have a Bounty Office, check the POI section for each island in the [Island Guide](/Islands/Overview).

## Bounty Heads

Every boss has a 1% chance to drop its head when killed. A bounty head is a special player head that carries the boss's skin along with its name, maximum health, and Belly reward at the moment it dropped.

## Rewards

The Belly reward is based on the boss's maximum health:

> Reward = Max Health × 10% (rounded down)

| Boss Max Health | Bounty Reward |
|----------------:|--------------:|
| 50              | 5 Belly       |
| 500             | 50 Belly      |
| 5,000           | 500 Belly     |
| 50,000          | 5,000 Belly   |

The reward is locked into the head when it drops, so turning in an older head always pays out the value it was created with.

## Claiming a Bounty

Speak to a Bounty Officer and place a bounty head into the head slot. The panel will show the target's name, health, and reward. Press Claim Bounty to receive the Belly and consume the head. Only one head can be turned in at a time.

## Active vs. Claimed Bounties

The Bounty Office tracks two lists per player:

- Active Bounties: bosses you haven't claimed yet.
- Claimed Bounties: bosses you have claimed at least once.

Each boss counts once toward your progress. Bosses respawn, so you can keep farming the same boss for heads and Belly, but only the first claim of each boss moves it from Active to Claimed.
