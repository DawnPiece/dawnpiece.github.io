# Market

Markets are server-wide player marketplaces found at various islands. Every Market Broker NPC shares the same global market, meaning listings and orders are accessible from any island with a Broker NPC. To see which islands have a Market, check the POI section for each island in the [Island Guide](/Islands/Overview).

All transactions use your Bank Account, physical Belly is never accepted. Market deposits bypass the usual 5% banking transaction fee.

## Listings (Browse & Buy)

The default market view shows all active item listings sorted by least time remaining. You can search by name or filter by category: Weapons, Armor, Devil Fruits, Fragments, Resources, Utility Items.

Bid: Enter an amount higher than the current bid (minimum bid is shown in the confirmation window). Money is reserved immediately. If you're outbid, your funds are returned via Messages. You cannot bid on your own listing.

Buy Now: Instantly purchase the item for the listed price. This closes the auction and refunds all previous bidders.

## List Items (Sell)

Place an item into the slot to list it for auction.

Set a Starting Bid and optionally a Buy Now Price. Listings last 7 days with a 100 Belly listing fee.

Bound items (quest items, misc items, skin shards, skin orbs, etc.) cannot be listed.

## Orders (Browse Buy Orders)

View all active buy orders. These are requests from players wanting to purchase specific items.

Sell: Fulfill all or part of a buy order. You'll receive payment for the items you provide. Partial fulfillment is supported. For example, if 2 Fragments are requested, you can sell 1.

## Place Order (Request Items)

Create a buy order requesting items from other players. Select the item, set quantity and price per item, then confirm.

The total cost (Quantity × Price Per Item) plus the 100 Belly listing fee is placed in escrow, ensuring you have the funds to back your request. Orders last 7 days.

## Messages

Every market action creates a message in your Messages tab. Nothing is delivered automatically so you must claim it.

| Message Type     | What You Get |
|------------------|---|
| Purchased Item   | Won an auction or bought via Buy Now. Claim to receive item. |
| Item Sold        | Your auction sold. Claim to deposit earnings (No 5% Fee). |
| Auction Expired  | No one bought your item. Claim to get it back. |
| Outbid Refund    | You were outbid. Claim to recover your reserved money. |
| Buy Order Item   | Someone fulfilled your buy order. Claim to receive items. |
| Buy Order Payment| You sold to a buy order. Claim to receive payment. |
| Buy Order Refund | Your buy order expired. Claim to recover remaining escrow. |

Items go to your inventory first, then bank storage if inventory is full.
