# Resource Merchant

Resource Merchants are locations found at various islands where you can sell Stone, Wood, and Fish resources for Belly and purchase Treasure Keys. To see which islands have a Resource Merchant, check the POI section for each island in the [Island Guide](/Islands/Overview). Please reference the [Harvesting](/General/Harvesting) section for information related to the harvesting system related to resources.

The merchant's shop has four tabs:

- Stone: sell stone resources
- Wood: sell wood resources
- Fish: sell fish resources
- Keys: craft treasure keys

## Selling Resources

Every resource is worth Belly based on its tier. The value doubles with each tier, starting at 1 Belly for Tier 1.

| Tier | Stone Resource  | Wood Resource    | Fish Resource | Belly |
|------|-----------------|------------------|---------------|-------|
| 1    | Stone Bit       | Wood Twig        | Minnow        | 1     |
| 2    | Stone Fragments | Wood Stick       | Clownfish     | 2     | 
| 3    | Small Stone     | Wood Branch      | Tuna          | 4     |
| 4    | Stone Clump     | Bundle of Sticks | Anglerfish    | 8     |
| 5    | Stone Brick     | Wood Log         | Octopus       | 16    |
| 6    | Stone Slate     | Wood Stump       | Shark         | 32    |
| 7    | Stone Slab      | Wood Bunch       | Whale         | 64    |
| 8    | Stone Bundle    | Wood Bundle      | Sea Serpent   | 128   |

Type the quantity you want to sell into the card's quantity field and confirm. The total Belly is calculated as quantity × per-item value.

## Treasure Keys

Combine resources and a shard to purchase a Treasure Key. Only resource tiers 1, 2, 4, 6, and 8 are used for keys; tiers 3, 5, and 7 are still sold for Belly but are not used in key recipes. When buying a key, the merchant checks both your inventory and Bank Storage. The required resources and shard can come from either location, combined together.

| Key       | Stone Resource     | Wood Resource       | Fish Resource | Shard    |
|-----------|--------------------|---------------------|---------------|----------|
| Common    | 64 Stone Bit       | 64 Wood Twig        | 64 Minnow     | Common   |
| Uncommon  | 64 Stone Fragments | 64 Wood Stick       | 64 Clownfish  | Uncommon | 
| Rare      | 64 Stone Clump     | 64 Bundle of Sticks | 64 Anglerfish | Rare     |
| Cosmetic  | 64 Stone Slate     | 64 Wood Stump       | 64 Shark      | Epic     |
| Legendary | 64 Stone Bundle    | 64 Wood Bundle      | 64 Sea Serpent| Legendary|
