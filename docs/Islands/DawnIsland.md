# Dawn Island Guide

This island is automatically accessible from Spawn Island. Speak to the Teleporter to warp to Foosha Village and begin questing!

## Main Quest

To start the Main Quest, speak to Luffy (DAWN) near the island teleport location.

| Quest Code| Quest Name               | Quest Objective|
|-----------|--------------------------|----------------|
| A01       | Pirates Drank The Booze! |Defeat Higuma and 5 Bandits on the west side of Foosha Village by the coast.|
| A02       | The Adventure Begins!    |Talk to Shanks (FV) at Partys Bar to complete the questline.|

> Item Rewards: 35 Belly, Common Shard, 8 Senbei<br>
> Bounty Reward: 35,000
> Note: Completing this quest unlocks Alvida's Hideout. Speak to Shanks (DAWN) or the Teleporter at any location to access the next island.

## B Quest

To start the B Quest, speak to Rigurd (DAWN) at the the fork of the Goa Kingdom Road and Dadan Family Hideout Road.

| Quest Code| Quest Name                  | Quest Objective|
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------|
| B01       | Find The Caravan!           |Find the Foosha Village Caravan along the road to Goa Kingdom to complete the quest.|
| B02       | Trouble With The Caravan!   |Talk to Cyrus (DAWN) by the wagon to complete the quest.|
| B03       | Defeat The Bandits!         |Defeat 3 Bandits and the Bandit Captain around the wagon to complete the quest.|
| B04       | Catch Up To The Caravan!    |Find the Main Caravan along the road to Goa Kingdom to complete the quest.|
| B05       | Hungry Wolves!              |Defeat 5 Mountain Wolves around the wagon to complete the quest.|
| B06       | The Caravan Guard!          |Talk to Leon (DAWN) by the wagon to complete the quest.|
| B07       | Find The Watchtower!        |Find the Abandoned Watchtower along the road to Goa Kingdom to complete the quest.|
| B08       | Clear The Watchtower!       |Defeat 3 Bandit Archers and the Bandit Captain around the watchtower to complete the quest.|
| B09       | Follow The Caravan!         |Find the Goa Kingdom Caravan nearby the Goa Kingdom entrance to complete the quest.|
| B10       | At Last, The Caravan Leader!|Talk to Roland (DAWN) nearby the Goa Kingdom entrance to complete the questline.|

> Item Rewards: 75 Belly, Common Shard, 8 Senbei<br>
> Bounty Reward: 75,000

## D Quest

To start the D Quest, speak to Young Luffy (DAWN) behind the Dadan Family Hideout.

| Quest Code| Quest Name                    | Quest Objective|
|-----------|-----------                    |-----------|
| D01       | Be Friends With Ace!          |Talk to Ace (GK) on the edge of the Gray Terminal to complete the quest.|
| D02       | Robbing The Bluejam Pirates!  |Talk to Sabo (GK) on the edge of the Gray Terminal to complete the quest.|
| D03       | The Bluejam Crew!             |Defeat 3 Bluejam Pirates and Porchemy in the Gray Terminal.|
| D04       | Dine & Dash!                  |Talk to Waiter (GK) in the restaurant to complete the quest.|
| D05       | Sabo’s Family!                |Talk to Outlook III (GK) in Sabo's House to complete the quest.|

| D07       | Save Ace & Luffy!             |Find Captive Ace (GK) and Captive Luffy (GK) in the Gray Terminal.|
