# Food

The DawnPiece Mod has a variety of food items from the One Piece World. The goal of food is to restore your hunger, stamina and health. Food can be bought at [Restaurants](/POI/Restaurant) throughout the world.

## Food Tiers

Health, Stamina and Hunger restoration as well as Eating Time depend on the tier of the food. Please see the chart below:

| Food Tier | Health | Hunger | Stamina | Eating Time |
|-----------|--------|--------|---------|-----------  |
| Common    | 2      | 1      | 1       | 0.5 Seconds |
| Uncommon  | 4      | 2      | 2       | 1 Second    |
| Rare      | 8      | 4      | 4       | 1.5 Seconds |
| Epic      | 16     | 8      | 8       | 2 Seconds   |
| Legendary | 32     | 16     | 16      | 2.5 Seconds |
| Mythical  | 64     | 32     | 32      | 3 Seconds   |
